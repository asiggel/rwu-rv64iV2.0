X-FAB \textbf{XH035} 350\,nm (0.35\,\textmu m) technology.

X-FAB's XH035 is a 0.35\,\textmu m Modular Mixed-Signal / High-Voltage CMOS technology,
designed for combined analog, digital, and mixed-signal circuit design.
The digital standard-cell library used for this design is the \texttt{D\_CELLS} library
(\texttt{xh035-D\_CELLS-liberty\_MOS}), which supplies the Liberty (\texttt{.lib}) timing
and power views for synthesis.
On-chip SRAM (I-Cache, D-Cache, and Scratchpad; see Section~\ref{sec:memfunc01}) is generated
with the X-FAB XH035 SPRAM (Single-Port RAM) compiler, available directly from the
X-FAB design-kit portal.

The nominal core voltage is 3.3\,V (a 2.2\,V low-voltage core option exists but is not used
in this design).
